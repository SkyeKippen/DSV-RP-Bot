- SKA accused BERTI of systemic deception and past atrocities, while threatening any who support the alliance.
- Hestia Space Works (HSW) established itself as an independent industrial and military power, unveiling the Hestia MK4 Shipyard.
- SKA and HSW engaged in escalating ideological and personal conflict; SKA distrusted HSW’s intentions, while HSW dismissed SKA’s theatrics and centralized vision.
- Despite shared disdain for BERTI, SKA and HSW repeatedly clashed over values, strategy, and trust — culminating in HSW blocking SKA from open comms.

- HSW claimed no territorial ownership over Tadara, asserting a stance of defense and decentralization while offering limited collaboration.
- Diplomatic strain grew as PNX urged HSW to be mindful of perception while relocating to Plunia and opening new trade routes.
- An encrypted transmission from the mysterious NEXUS fleet warned of new, undefined hostilities; HSW and others received garbled or cryptic messages.
- PNX's Mobius searched for a lost contact, signaling the return of past storylines tied to the Hellspawn conflict.
