- The Sansha's Nation (SNT), via Brassmaster Fang, proposed peace to the Soell Federal Militia (SFM) and offered gold as a gesture, claiming admiration for their military strength.
- Skye Meadows announced the formation of Skye’s Ascendency (SKA), declaring open hostility against the Berruti Alliance and urging others to rise against it.
- SKA accused BERTI of disconnecting star systems from the Jump Bridge Network and referenced past atrocities covered up by BERTI.
- SKA invited rebels and allies to open secure channels with SKA and threatened destruction for those who supported BERTI.
- SFM's Rear Admiral Manning rejected SNT’s peace offer, referencing past attacks and declared war, threatening to destroy the SNT fleet.
- Hestia Space Works (HSW) completed the Hestia MK4 Shipyard, establishing themselves as a powerful independent shipbuilding force.
- SKA criticized HSW’s intentions, clarifying that SKA did not seek to replace BERTI but questioned HSW’s apparent desire for dominance.
- Skye challenged HSW's leader Jax Soren, questioning his trust and vision regarding SKA's movement and goals.
- Jax Soren of HSW responded that he respects strength through individuality, not subservience, and declined to follow SKA’s leadership model.
- HSW emphasized collaboration only on mutual terms and warned against centralized control, drawing a contrast with BERTI's secretive structure.
- A distress signal was sent by Kieran Mobius of Phoenix Manufacturing, requesting assistance while stranded in uncharted space.
- SKA questioned HSW's commitment to mutual support, labeling them “dangerous” for prioritizing independence over unity in warfare.
- Skye Meadows expressed distrust toward HSW, stating their decentralization makes them unpredictable, and warned them not to interfere in SKA’s mission.
- HSW clarified their stance, claiming they fight for survival, not dominance, and emphasized resilience through decentralized strength.
- Both SKA and HSW acknowledged they do not trust one another, with tensions escalating to veiled threats of open conflict.
- HSW reasserted they had not claimed ownership of Tadara, only that they operate and defend there.
- A BERTI assault on Tadara led to serious damage to HSW facilities, but was ultimately repelled. HSW recovered unfamiliar tech from the wreckage.
- Skye accused HSW of hiding intentions, exaggerating reports, and preparing to fill the power vacuum if BERTI collapses.
- Jax Soren responded that HSW has no desire for empire, only survival, and warned Skye against attacking them.
- Phoenix Manufacturing (PNX) expressed concern about the true motive behind BERTI’s attack and emphasized the risk of destabilizing the region further.
- Mobius of PNX criticized both SKA and HSW for their ongoing conflict and suggested cooperation to uncover BERTI’s true motives.
- HSW and PNX exchanged diplomatic messages; HSW warned PNX against spreading misinformation, and PNX urged HSW to be mindful of their appearance as a growing power.
- PNX announced relocation to Plunia for stability and trade, while monitoring future threats from BERTI or tensions among allies.
- SKA continued accusing HSW of recklessness and lacking moral alignment, while asserting that passion—not experience—would lead to BERTI’s defeat.
- HSW responded by doubling down on pragmatism, stating they had not struck BERTI with full force, and dismissed SKA's tactics as reactive and theatrical.
- Skye threatened retaliation if provoked and challenged HSW’s reliance on replaceable metal over human cost, asking if commanders were just as expendable.
- Tensions escalated with veiled threats from both SKA and HSW, culminating in HSW blocking SKA from open wide-band communication.
- PNX maintained neutrality but urged SKA not to underestimate BERTI and warned of coming escalation if BA was probing defenses.
- Skye reaffirmed her readiness for a fight, stating her mobility and cover under FORGE territory make her hard to target.
- HSW reiterated they never claimed Tadara as their own, only that they defend it as their home and operate quietly by design.
- Mobius (PNX) warned HSW about how their position might appear to others and cautioned against overreach, despite acknowledging HSW's intent.
- BA’s recovered tech was being studied at a newly launched HSW research lab, with collaboration offered to neutral factions.
- A strange, encrypted transmission hinted at the arrival of a hostile group called the NEXUS, identified as a spec ops fleet refusing communication.
- An unknown party broadcasted cryptic and aggressive responses to garbled, regret-filled, and waking transmissions, signaling growing unrest.
- PNX reached out to search for Aelsius, a lost contact from the Hellspawn era, marking a shift toward personal history amid mounting tension.
